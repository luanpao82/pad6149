## C3 Reflection Paper (2–3 pages)

Write 2–3 pages on communicating and interacting with a diverse and changing workforce in the nonprofit sector. Reflect on how your **HR case study (Week 5)** and **financial/budget analysis** demonstrate this competency. Address:

- What makes communication across a diverse workforce and citizenry difficult, and what have you learned to do about it?
- How does financial communication build (or erode) trust with stakeholders?
- Where do your two artifacts reinforce each other?

Add 1–2 scholarly sources via **Google Scholar**; cite in APA 7th.
