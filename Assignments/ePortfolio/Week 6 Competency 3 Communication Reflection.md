## What Is This Assignment?

This week's module covers **marketing and the images of nonprofit organizations** — how nonprofits communicate with diverse constituencies, donors, and the public. This short reflection connects that material to Competency 3 and to your own evidence.

## Reflection Prompt (1–2 pages)

Drawing on this week's readings and your HR evidence from Week 5, address:

- How do nonprofits communicate their mission and identity to a *diverse and changing* citizenry?
- Where does communication most often break down across cultural, linguistic, or demographic difference — and what closes the gap?
- From your own coursework or experience, give one concrete example of communicating productively across difference. What worked, and why?
- How does effective external communication (marketing, image) connect to effective internal communication (workforce, volunteers)?

This is a reflective piece — first-person, evidence-grounded, concise.
