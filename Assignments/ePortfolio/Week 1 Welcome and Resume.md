## What Is This Assignment?

Your ePortfolio opens with a **Welcome section** — the first thing a prospective employer, board, or funder sees. It frames who you are, why you entered the nonprofit sector, and what you intend to do with your degree. This week you draft that statement and attach the resume that anchors it.

## Write Your Welcome Statement

Address each of the following:

- Provide an overview of the importance of nonprofit management education.
- Why did you pursue this particular academic path?
- How has it enhanced your knowledge of the nonprofit sector and of nonprofit management and leadership?
- What do you plan to do next in your career?
- How do you plan to use your Master of Nonprofit Management degree?

Write in a confident, professional first-person voice — this is a public-facing introduction, not an academic essay.

## Attach Your Resume

Upload a current resume that includes relevant **work or volunteer experience in the nonprofit sector**. This becomes evidence in your portfolio that your competencies are grounded in practice, not only coursework.

> [!tip] Make it real
> Use **Census Reporter** or your own field knowledge to add a sentence of sector context to your Welcome ("the sector employs X% of the regional workforce…"). Specifics signal seriousness.
