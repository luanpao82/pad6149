## What Is This Assignment?

This course treats AI fluency as a professional competency. Throughout the portfolio you've used AI tools to recall, synthesize, and draft. This week you **document and reflect on that use** — and give the whole portfolio a polish pass before the final assembly.

## Part I: AI-Use Reflection (2–3 pages)

Reflect critically on how you used AI across this portfolio. Address:

- **Where did AI genuinely help?** (recalling prior work, synthesizing across artifacts, tightening prose, finding sources)
- **Where did it fall short or mislead?** (hallucinated citations, generic reflections, missing your actual voice)
- **What did you have to own?** Every claim, citation, and judgment is yours — describe where your judgment overrode the tool.
- **What is the ethical line** between AI-assisted work and academic dishonesty, in your own words?
- How will you use AI as a nonprofit professional — for grants, donor communications, analysis — responsibly?

## Part II: Portfolio Polish Pass

Review every section for:

- [ ] Consistent design, fonts, and navigation
- [ ] Every artifact has a competency-claiming caption
- [ ] All five competency reflections are complete and proofread
- [ ] All citations in APA 7th edition
- [ ] No AI-generated text left in a generic, un-owned voice

> [!warning] Ethical AI use
> You must author, direct, critically evaluate, and take ownership of all AI-assisted work. Submitting raw AI output without your own analysis and revision is academic dishonesty. Label any AI-generated images.
