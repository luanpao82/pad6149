## What Is This Assignment?

An ePortfolio is an *electronic* portfolio — a website that presents your evidence and reflections in one professional place you can share with employers. This week you build the shell. You'll fill it with evidence and reflections over Weeks 4–13.

## Build the Structure

Create a site with these pages (or sections):

1. **Welcome** — your Week 1 statement + resume link
2. **Competency 1** — Lead and manage *(placeholder)*
3. **Competency 2** — Analyze & solve problems *(placeholder)*
4. **Competency 3** — Communicate with a diverse workforce *(placeholder)*
5. **Competency 4** — Public service & governance *(placeholder)*
6. **Competency 5** — Policy process *(placeholder)*

Each competency page should already include a one-line statement of the competency and an empty "Evidence" and "Reflection" area you'll fill later.

> [!example] Free site builders that work well for ePortfolios
> - **[Google Sites](https://sites.google.com)** — simplest; no cost, easy uploads, instant publishing
> - **[Wix](https://wix.com)** / **[Carrd](https://carrd.co)** — more design control, free tier
> - **[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com)** — if you want a developer-style portfolio

> [!tip] Design like a professional
> Keep it clean and readable — consistent fonts, generous spacing, your name and a headshot or simple logo. A recruiter should understand who you are in 15 seconds.
