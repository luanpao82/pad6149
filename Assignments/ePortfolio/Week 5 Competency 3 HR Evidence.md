## What Is This Section?

Competency 3 is about managing and communicating with a **diverse and changing workforce**. This week you add the first of its two pieces of evidence: your human resources case study. (The second piece — a financial/budget analysis — and the full C3 reflection come in Week 7.)

## Evidence to Include

| Measurement | Standard evidence | What it should show |
|---|---|---|
| Human resource management | **PAD 6417 HR Case Study** | You can analyze and propose thoughtful solutions to an HR case addressing a diverse workforce and citizenry |

Curate the artifact onto your Competency 3 page with a **3–4 sentence caption** that explains: what the assignment was, the workforce challenge it addressed, the solution you proposed, and which competency measurement it demonstrates.

> [!tip] Strengthen the caption
> A good caption is a mini argument: "This case demonstrates my ability to… because I…". Don't just describe the assignment — claim the competency.
