# Choose Your Capstone Path

**Read this before Week 3. Your choice is due before graded work begins in Week 3 and is binding for the semester.**

This capstone lets you choose **how** you demonstrate mastery of the five program competencies. Everyone follows the same weekly readings and lectures and completes the common **Leadership Case Study** — but your weekly deliverables follow one of two paths. Both paths are graded against the same standards, carry identical point values, and total **80 points** (path deliverables 50 + the shared Leadership Case 30). Graded work begins in **Week 3**; Weeks 1–2 are for orientation, readings, and choosing your path. Both paths end with a **live website and a 5–8 minute video due December 3**.

---

## Option A — ePortfolio

You curate and reflect on the strongest evidence of your mastery from across your MNM program, assembling a professional ePortfolio organized around the five NASPAA competencies. Each week you submit one piece — an artifact of evidence plus a reflection.

**Best fit if:** you are entering or advancing in the job market and want a portfolio URL for your resume, LinkedIn, or interviews; you want to consolidate and deepen what you have already learned; you do your best work through reflective, analytical writing.

**You finish with:** a polished ePortfolio website documenting your mastery, ready to share with employers.

## Option B — Build the Petco Love Lost Start-Up

You build a real start-up nonprofit — jointly created by **Halifax Humane Society** and **Petco Love** — whose single purpose is to operate the **Petco Love Lost** free, AI-powered lost-and-found-pet program well across Volusia County. Each week you pull one piece of your prior coursework and adapt it to this real case (board, staffing, budget, strategy, programs, advocacy), and each week's page walks you through the specific AI tools, prompts, and check-and-revise steps for that deliverable. The organization is fictional, but the partners, the problem, and the data are real.

**Best fit if:** you want to found, lead, or consult for an organization and want the experience of assembling every piece; you want work you can point to in an interview — an organizational plan and pitch built from a real partnership case, seen by the partners themselves; you want hands-on, guided practice with AI tools (writing, design, budgeting, video, and web).

**You finish with:** a fully developed website and organizational blueprint for the start-up. After the term, completed sites are shared with Halifax Humane Society and Petco Love and may inform how the program is actually operated in Volusia County.

---

## What's the Same on Both Paths

- The weekly readings, lectures, and modules
- The common **Leadership Case Study** (30 pts, Week 11) — the single largest assignment
- The same grading standards and total points (80)
- The same AI policy — use AI as working nonprofit leaders do, but author, evaluate, and own every output
- A live website and a required 5–8 minute video, due December 3

## How to Choose

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Before Week 3 (graded work begins Week 3) |
| **How** | Submit the "Capstone Path Declaration" (or follow the instructor's stated method) indicating Option A or Option B |
| **Binding?** | Yes — your choice holds for the semester |
| **Unsure?** | Option A suits a strong completed-coursework portfolio you want to present; Option B suits hands-on building of a real start-up with a real client. Both satisfy every competency and lead to the same degree outcome. Contact the instructor to talk it through. |

> [!info] After you choose
> You will be added to your path's section so that only your path's weekly assignments appear for you. The other path's assignments will not show in your to-do list or count toward your grade.
