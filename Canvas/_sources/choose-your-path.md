# Choose Your Capstone Path

**Read this in Week 1. Your choice is due by the end of Week 1 and is binding for the semester.**

This capstone lets you choose **how** you demonstrate mastery of the five program competencies. Everyone follows the same weekly readings and lectures and completes the common **Leadership Case Study** — but your weekly deliverables follow one of two paths. Both paths are graded on the same competency rubric, carry identical point values, and total 100 points.

---

## Option A — ePortfolio

You curate and reflect on the strongest evidence of your mastery from across your MNM program, assembling a professional ePortfolio organized around the five NASPAA competencies. Each week you submit one piece — an artifact of evidence plus a reflection.

**Best fit if:** you have a strong body of completed MNM coursework you want to consolidate and present professionally as you enter the job market.

**You finish with:** a polished ePortfolio website documenting your mastery, ready to share with employers.

## Option B — Build Your Nonprofit

You build a complete, real nonprofit organization from the ground up — mission, board, staffing plan, programs, budget, marketing identity, financials, and advocacy strategy — adapting your prior coursework into a living organizational blueprint and public website.

**Best fit if:** you have a cause or organization you want to develop, and possibly launch.

**You finish with:** a fully developed nonprofit website and organizational blueprint.

---

## What's the Same on Both Paths

- The weekly readings, lectures, and modules
- The common **Leadership Case Study** (20 pts, Week 11)
- The competency rubric and total points (100)
- A required final presentation (a portfolio walkthrough or a pitch video)

## How to Choose

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | By the end of Week 1 |
| **How** | Submit the "Capstone Path Declaration" (or follow the instructor's stated method) indicating Option A or Option B |
| **Binding?** | Yes — your choice holds for the semester |
| **Unsure?** | Option A suits a strong completed-coursework portfolio; Option B suits a cause you want to build. Both satisfy every competency and lead to the same degree outcome. Contact the instructor to talk it through. |

> [!info] After you choose
> You will be added to your path's section so that only your path's weekly assignments appear for you. The other path's assignments will not show in your to-do list or count toward your grade.
