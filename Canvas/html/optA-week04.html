What Is This Section?

This is your first full competency section (7 pts). You will assemble two pieces of evidence that demonstrate your public-service perspective and your grasp of nonprofit governance and ethics, then write a reflection connecting them.

Evidence to Include

Measurement Standard evidence What it should show
Governance & Codes of Ethics PAD 6237 Governance Case Study You can articulate the public-service perspective through the ASPA and AFP codes of ethics as applied to a nonprofit
Public-service motivation PAD 5145 Volunteer Motivation Literature Review You can apply the public-service perspective to the motivations of nonprofit volunteers

For each artifact, add a 2–3 sentence caption to your portfolio page explaining what it is, the course context, and which competency measurement it demonstrates. (No artifact yet? Substitute a justified alternate and note your completion plan — refer back to the competency self-assessment and evidence plan you completed during orientation.)

Reflection Paper (2–3 pages)

Write 2–3 pages on the public-service perspective in the nonprofit sector. Reflect on how the assignments you used as evidence allowed you to demonstrate — or learn about — this competency. Address:

  • How does the public-service perspective shape the way nonprofits should be governed?
  • What did the governance case teach you about the board's ethical and fiduciary role?
  • How do the codes of ethics (ASPA, AFP) apply in practice, and where are they hardest to honor?
  • What does volunteer motivation reveal about civil society's role in democratic governance?

Use Google Scholar to add 1–2 scholarly sources that deepen the reflection beyond the original assignments. Cite in APA 7th edition.
