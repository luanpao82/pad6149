What Is This Assignment?

This is the capstone of your capstone. Every competency is now evidenced and reflected. This week you assemble the finished ePortfolio — a single professional site that documents your mastery across all five NASPAA competencies — and present it, walking a viewer through your strongest work as if introducing yourself to an employer or board.

Part I: Assemble the Finished ePortfolio

Your portfolio must include:

Every section: two pieces of evidence, captioned, plus a 2–3 page reflection. Consistent, professional design throughout.

Part II: Present (5–8 minute video)

Record a 5–8 minute video in which you appear on camera and walk a viewer through your portfolio:

[0:00–1:00] Welcome — who you are, why nonprofit management, where you're headed. [1:00–6:00] Competencies — for each, show your strongest evidence and state, in one sentence, what it proves about you. [6:00–7:30] Synthesis — what the body of work says about you as a nonprofit leader. [7:30–8:00] Close — your portfolio URL and contact information.

This is your professional introduction. Treat it like the final round of a job interview.
