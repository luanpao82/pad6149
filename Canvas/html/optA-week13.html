What Is This Section?

The final competency section. You assemble two pieces of evidence on the policy process and advocacy, then write the reflection that completes your portfolio's competency coverage.

Evidence to Include

Measurement Standard evidence What it should show
Advocacy PAD 6142 Policy Advocacy Plan You can identify and apply the legal and ethical implications of advocacy in a nonprofit policy advocacy plan
Policy process PAD 5146 Policy Process Assignment You can describe the policy process and analyze methods of political advocacy

Curate both artifacts with competency-claiming captions on your Competency 5 page.

C5 Reflection Paper (2–3 pages)

Write 2–3 pages on the policy process in the nonprofit sector. Reflect on how your policy advocacy plan and policy process assignment demonstrate this competency. Address:

  • How do nonprofits legitimately participate in and shape the policy process?
  • Where are the legal and ethical limits of nonprofit advocacy (e.g., lobbying rules), and how do you navigate them?
  • What did your advocacy plan teach you about moving from analysis to political action?
  • Why does policy engagement matter to civil society and democratic governance?

Add 1–2 scholarly sources via Google Scholar; cite in APA 7th.
