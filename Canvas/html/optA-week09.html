What Is This Section?

Competency 1 — leadership and management — is the spine of the capstone. The strategic plan is one of the strongest, most comprehensive artifacts in your portfolio, so this week carries real weight (7 pts). You add it as evidence and write a focused analysis of it. (The second C1 piece — your Leadership Case Study — comes in Week 11, with the full C1 reflection.)

Evidence to Include

Measurement Standard evidence What it should show
Strategic planning & management PAD 6335 Strategic Plan You can develop a strategic plan that builds consensus among diverse stakeholders, focuses on mission, and is responsive to a changing environment

Strategic-Plan Analysis (~2 pages)

Rather than a full reflection (that comes Week 11), write a focused analysis of your own strategic plan as evidence of leadership and management:

  • What strategic challenge did the plan address, and what choices did it make?
  • How did the plan build consensus among diverse stakeholders and a volunteer board?
  • How is it responsive and innovative in the face of a changing environment?
  • With hindsight, what would you strengthen — and what does that tell you about your growth as a manager?
