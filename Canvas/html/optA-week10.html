C2 Reflection Paper (2–3 pages)

Write 2–3 pages on your ability to think critically, solve problems, and make wise decisions in the nonprofit sector. Reflect on how your volunteer case study (Week 8) and program evaluation plan demonstrate this competency. Address:

  • What does rigorous analysis look like in nonprofit practice, and how do your artifacts demonstrate it?
  • How do you move from data and diagnosis to a defensible decision?
  • Where did you have to weigh competing values or imperfect information?
  • How has your analytical practice matured across the program?

Add 1–2 scholarly sources via Google Scholar; cite in APA 7th.
