What Is This Section?

Competency 2 is your analytical and problem-solving competency. This week you add the first of its two pieces of evidence: your volunteer management case study. (The second piece — a program evaluation plan — and the full C2 reflection come in Week 10.)

Evidence to Include

Measurement Standard evidence What it should show
Volunteer management analysis PAD 5145 Volunteer Case Study You can conduct a volunteer management case study — diagnosing a problem and making a defensible decision

Curate the artifact with a 3–4 sentence caption that names the problem you analyzed, the decision you reached, and why it demonstrates critical thinking.

Show the thinking, not just the answer

Competency 2 is about how you reason. In your caption, foreground the analytical move — the diagnosis, the trade-off, the decision — not just the recommendation.
