What Is This Assignment?

Before you assemble evidence, you need a plan. This week you rate yourself against the five NASPAA competencies and decide, for each, which two artifacts you will use as evidence and why. This plan is your roadmap for Weeks 4–13.

Step 1: Self-Assessment

Rate your current mastery of each competency (Emerging / Developing / Proficient) and write 2–3 sentences explaining the rating.

# Competency
C1 Lead and manage in a dynamic, mission-driven nonprofit sector
C2 Analyze, synthesize, think critically, solve problems, and make decisions
C3 Communicate and interact productively with a diverse and changing workforce and citizenry
C4 Articulate and apply a public-service perspective emphasizing civil society and governance
C5 Participate in and contribute to the policy process

Step 2: Evidence Plan

For each competency, identify the two pieces of evidence you will submit and where each comes from. The standard sources are below; you may substitute an internship, volunteer, or work artifact if it demonstrates the competency.

Missing a piece of evidence?

If you have not yet completed one of the source courses, indicate when you will complete it and propose alternate documentation (a work product, internship deliverable, or volunteer artifact) that measures the same competency.

Grading Rubric (2 pts)

Criteria Points
Honest, specific self-assessment across all five competencies 1 pt
Complete evidence plan naming two artifacts per competency (or a justified alternate) 1 pt
