What Is This Assignment?

You've spent the semester building the organization on paper and on a website. This week you make it speak — the way a real launch speaks. A start-up that runs Petco Love Lost in Volusia County lives or dies on whether the public finds it, trusts it, and uses it. Every reunion begins with someone uploading a photo, and nobody uploads a photo of a program they've never heard of.

AI tools now let a one-person communications shop produce professional-quality copy, graphics, and video without a design team or a production budget. That is exactly the situation a new nonprofit is in. So this week you use AI to draft and design a launch content package for the start-up: reunion-story posts, lost-pet alerts, a registration-drive push, and a cross-channel launch announcement — then you do a final polish pass on the whole website before the pitch.

Course AI Policy: You Author It, You Own It

AI drafts; you author. Every post, caption, image, and script you submit must be read, corrected, and approved by you — the facts must be right, the tone must fit a shelter partnership, and nothing may misstate what Petco Love Lost actually does. You are responsible for the final product exactly as if you had typed every word yourself. "The AI wrote it" is never an excuse for an error. Throughout this page you'll add a one-line author's note to each piece stating what you changed and why — that note is the assignment, as much as the content itself.

The Case: Petco Love Lost Start-Up

Roughly 10 million dogs and cats go missing in the United States each year. Your start-up — jointly created by Halifax Humane Society and Petco Love — exists to run the Petco Love Lost program well across Volusia County, Florida (Daytona Beach and nine municipalities). Petco Love Lost is a free, AI-powered photo-matching database: upload a photo of a lost or found pet and it searches a national pool in seconds. It is now used by more than 3,000 shelters and rescues, with over 300,000 pets registered and more than 100,000 reunions to date. Halifax Humane Society shelters, treats, and rehomes nearly 15,000 animals a year and has partnered with Petco Love to bring the program to Volusia County in the first coordinated countywide deployment. As Halifax's CEO puts it, the technology "can help reunite lost pets within minutes — without them ever having to enter a shelter." This is the message your content has to carry.

The Case: Petco Love Lost Start-Up — Reunion Story Post

🐾 Bailey is home. Last Tuesday, a golden retriever slipped out of a backyard in Port Orange during a thunderstorm. His family did one thing right away: they uploaded his photo to Petco Love Lost — free, no account, 30 seconds. That same afternoon, a good Samaritan across town found a scared, soaked dog and uploaded his photo too. The system matched them in minutes. Bailey never spent a night in a shelter.

Roughly 10 million pets go missing in the U.S. every year. Most don't have a photo on file. Yours can. Register your pet's photo today — free — at [your org's Petco Love Lost link]. #PetcoLoveLost #VolusiaCounty

Author's note: The AI draft opened with a statistic; I moved the emotional hook first and the statistic last, and cut two hashtags. Reunion is illustrative, clearly plausible, not presented as a verified case.

Facts You May Not Bend

You may invent a plausible reunion, a pet's name, and a neighborhood. You may not contradict the canonical facts: it is a free tool, it does photo matching against a national pool, it is run with Halifax Humane Society and Petco Love, and it serves Volusia County. Do not invent match rates, dollar figures, or staff numbers and present them as fact. If you cite the 100,000+ reunions or 300,000+ registrations, keep those numbers exactly.

AI Image Generation Prompt

Use Canva Magic Media or Microsoft Designer (free with a Microsoft account):

A warm, natural-light photograph of a happy dog being hugged by its
owner on a front porch in coastal Florida. Hopeful, candid, documentary
style. Room at the top for text. No text in the image, no logos.

For anything data-driven (an impact stat card), don't generate a fake photo — use a Canva template and type in the real number (e.g., "More than 100,000 pets reunited").

AI Image Ethics

  • Do not present an AI-generated dog or person as a real, specific animal or client of the organization.
  • Never pair an AI-generated pet photo with a "reunion story" in a way that implies it is a documented case — a reader must not be misled into thinking a real pet was photographed.
  • Label AI-generated imagery as such on your website if it could be mistaken for a real photo.
  • Prefer AI for templates, backgrounds, and graphic elements; use real (or clearly illustrative) photos for anything that reads as evidence.

Part 3: Create a 30–60 Second Launch Video

Produce a short video that introduces the start-up and the free tool — the kind that plays on the homepage, at a Halifax adoption event, or in a grant application.

What your video should include:

  • The start-up's name and logo (from Week 6)
  • The problem in one line (10 million pets go missing a year; most have no photo on file)
  • What the tool does (free, AI photo matching, results in seconds)
  • The partnership credibility (created with Halifax Humane Society and Petco Love)
  • A call to action ("Register your pet's photo — free")

Recommended Tools (all free tiers):

Option A: Canva Video (easiest) — search "nonprofit video," pick a template, drop in your script lines, brand colors, and images; add free music; export MP4.

Option B: Lumen5 (lumen5.com) — paste your launch announcement text; Lumen5's AI builds scenes, picks footage, and adds transitions; customize and export (free tier watermark).

Option C: InVideo AI (invideo.io) — describe the start-up in a prompt; InVideo drafts a full video with stock footage and music; edit scenes and export.

AI Tip: Video Script

Part 4: Cross-Channel Launch & Scheduling

Real launches don't post once — they post on a schedule, across channels, in a sequence. Plan a one-week launch calendar for the four pieces from Part 1, then show you know how the scheduling tools work.

AI Tip: Build the Calendar, Then Schedule It

Ask ChatGPT or Claude to turn your four pieces into a schedule:

I have four posts for a nonprofit launch: a reunion story, a
lost-pet alert template, a registration-drive push, and a launch
announcement. Build a 7-day posting calendar across Facebook,
Instagram, and LinkedIn for a Volusia County audience. For each
slot give day, time, channel, which post, and a one-line reason.

Then load the posts into a free scheduler so they publish on their own:

Tool What It Does
Meta Business Suite Free; schedule Facebook + Instagram posts together — the two channels most likely to reach local pet owners
Buffer Free tier; schedule across multiple channels from one dashboard
Canva Content Planner Design and schedule a post in the same place

You do not have to publish live to real accounts (this is a class project — see the disclosure rules below). You do have to include your launch calendar in the submitted content package, and a screenshot of a post staged in one scheduler earns credit toward the video demo.
