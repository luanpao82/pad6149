Leadership Case Study & C1 Reflection

Due: November 2, 2026 · 30 pts · 2,000–2,500 word essay + 2–3 page reflection

NASPAA Competency 1: Lead and manage in a dynamic, mission-driven nonprofit sector

Common requirement — both paths

The Leadership Case Study is required of every student, on either capstone path. On the ePortfolio path you write the analytical case essay, add it as your second piece of Competency 1 evidence, and write the C1 reflection that completes the competency. There is no website "Our Approach" page requirement on this path.

Part I: The Leadership Case Study Essay (2,000–2,500 words)

Identify and analyze a real nonprofit leadership challenge in a field that interests you. Find 3–5 recent news articles (2024–2026) about the issue, read across them for patterns, and translate your analysis into a leadership argument. Your essay should address:

  1. What is happening? Read across the articles for patterns — not article-by-article summary.
  2. Why is this happening? Root-cause analysis, integrating frameworks from your MNM coursework.
  3. Who is responding? Identify at least two organizations addressing the challenge — their strategies, successes, and gaps.
  4. What's missing? The gap your analysis reveals that current responses don't address.
  5. How would you lead through this? As a nonprofit leader, what concrete, operational strategy would you pursue?

Use Perplexity.ai and Google News to find articles; Google Scholar for academic grounding; Census Reporter for local data. Cite all sources in APA 7th edition.

Part II: C1 Reflection (2–3 pages)

Write 2–3 pages on leadership and management in the nonprofit sector. Reflect on how your strategic plan (Week 9) and this leadership case study together demonstrate Competency 1 — your ability to lead and manage in a dynamic, mission-driven sector. What has the program taught you about leading through uncertainty?

Add the finished essay to your portfolio's Competency 1 page alongside your strategic plan.

Grading Rubric (30 pts)

Criteria Points
Articles well-selected; essay shows pattern analysis, not summary 6 pts
Root-cause analysis integrates MNM frameworks and local data 7 pts
Stakeholder analysis identifies real organizations with specific assessment 5 pts
"What's missing" is sharp and evidence-grounded 4 pts
C1 reflection meaningfully connects strategic plan + case study to the competency 5 pts
APA 7th, professional writing 3 pts

Independent Work

This assignment is completed independently. Do not discuss the case or your essay with other students.
